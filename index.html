Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Stallion Nexus.

Do I have to take every load you offer?
Absolutely not. We operate on a strict 100% Non-Forced Dispatch policy. We hunt for the highest paying loads, present you with the best options, but you are the absolute decision-maker. You choose where you want to drive.
How is your pricing structured?
We offer highly flexible pricing tailored to your equipment type and volume. You can select either a highly competitive percentage-based rate or a flat weekly fee. There are absolutely zero hidden costs and no mandatory long-term contracts.
What documents do I need to get started?
The onboarding process is very simple. We just need three basic documents to get you set up: your Active MC Authority document, a signed W-9 form, and your Certificate of Insurance (COI).
Do you work with new MC Authorities?
Yes! While some brokers hesitate to work with fresh MCs, we have an established network of premium brokers who trust our management and gladly accept new carriers. We will help you build your reputation fast.
How does the E-Commerce scaling work?
Think of it as diversifying your income portfolio. While your trucks generate active logistics revenue, our dedicated digital team builds, manages, and scales automated E-Commerce stores (eBay, Amazon, TikTok) under your ownership, generating passive wealth streams.
Who handles my insurance claims and audits?
We have a dedicated back-office team and an exclusive network of insurance professionals. We handle your COI requests instantly and provide full support during claims, disputes, and compliance audits so you don't have to stress.
